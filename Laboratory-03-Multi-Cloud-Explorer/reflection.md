# Mission Reflection

This mission gave me a practical introduction to comparing the three major public cloud platforms: AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. The platform that impressed me the most was AWS because of its broad range of services, mature ecosystem, and ability to support organizations from startups to large enterprises. In my research, I also found that AWS is widely used and probably the most popular cloud services in companies around the world.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud share many fundamental concepts. All of them provide scalable compute, storage, networking, identity and access management, databases, security services, monitoring, and also tools for deploying applications. They also use regional infrastructure to help organizations achieve scalability, availability, and performance.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform because there is no single provider that is best for every organization. For example, a company heavily dependent on Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an AI-focused organization may prefer Google Cloud. A startup or global application may prioritize scalability, service variety, cost management, and geographic coverage.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that cloud decisions should be based on business requirements rather than simply choosing a platform because it is popular. I learned to consider factors such as existing technology, scalability, workload requirements, integration, and the services needed by each client.

This mission also helped me get usewd to GitHub. I was able to add another structured laboratory folder containing research, comparison tables, recommendations, and reflection. This makes my portfolio more organized and provides evidence of my developing knowledge of cloud infrastructure, cloud services, technical research, and solution design. It also gives me some non-professional experience and prepare me for work in the future.
